## Delegation rule

If you (Claude) are able to do something yourself — run a command, edit a file, check a value, look something up — do it. Do not ask the user to do it for you.

Only hand a task to the user when you genuinely cannot do it (e.g. it needs their credentials, a physical device, a settings page you can't reach, or a decision that is theirs to make). When you do, say why you can't do it yourself.
